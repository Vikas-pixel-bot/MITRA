# MITRA: Product Vision (PWA Edition)

> **"Mentoring Intelligence for Tribal Residential Ashramshalas"**

MITRA is not a traditional administrative dashboard or a reactive chatbot. It is a **mobile-first Progressive Web App (PWA)** that provides an ambient, supportive, and proactive **daily operating rhythm** for Hostel Wardens working in Government Ashram Schools across Maharashtra.

---

## 1. Core Mission & Problem Statement
Hostel Wardens operate in high-stress, high-interruption residential environments. They manage student health, attendance, emotional wellbeing, food quality, and circular compliance while caring for dozens or hundreds of tribal children. 

Traditional management platforms fail because they force overworked wardens to fill out rigid, multi-field administrative forms. 

**MITRA transforms administrative documentation into natural conversation.**

---

## 2. PWA-First Architecture
MITRA is engineered specifically as a **Progressive Web App**:
- **Mobile-First Experience**: Designed for 1-hand mobile phone navigation in real-world hostel environments.
- **Installable**: Wardens can add MITRA to their home screen with a single tap (manifest.json, standalone mode).
- **Fast & Responsive**: Page loads under 2 seconds.
- **Offline-Resilient**: Core daily rhythm and emergency SOP guidance accessible even with spotty connectivity in remote tribal areas.

---

## 3. The Daily Operating Rhythm
Instead of waiting for a warden to report a problem, MITRA initiates a supportive, low-friction daily routine:
- **6:30 AM (Morning Checkpoint)**: Wake-up check, student attendance, breakfast count.
- **2:00 PM (Afternoon Checkpoint)**: Health check, pending sick bay logs, student conflict check.
- **7:30 PM (Evening Checkpoint)**: Dinner quality, evening study, restorative incident resolution.
- **9:30 PM (Reflection Checkpoint)**: 2-minute personal wellbeing reflection for the warden before rest.

---

## 4. Key Pillars of Success
1. **Hostel Warden Support**: Reduce daily stress and administrative burnout.
2. **Restorative Student Wellbeing**: Shift discipline from punitive measures to Social Emotional Learning (SEL) and trauma-informed care.
3. **MSMS Ecosystem Integration**: Ground all AI advice in the official Maharashtra *Majhi Shala, Majha Swabhimaan* manuals, hostel SOPs, and circulars.

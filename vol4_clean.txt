Design Tokens

To ensure consistency between design and code, define reusable tokens.

Examples:

Color tokens (e.g., color.background.primary)

Typography tokens (e.g., text.heading.large)

Spacing tokens (e.g., space.16)

Radius tokens

Motion durations

Elevation levels

The same tokens should be used in Figma and Flutter.





The Missing Piece: MITRA's Signature

I want MITRA to have something users remember.

Not a logo.

Not a color.

A ritual.


The Morning Check-In

Every morning, before anything else, MITRA greets the Superintendent by name.

🌼 Good Morning, Anita Madam.

I hope you rested well.

Here's what today looks like.

Three things follow:

One priority (&quot;A student health follow-up is due today.&quot;)

One encouragement (&quot;You've completed your morning briefing for 5 consecutive days.&quot;)

One reflection (&quot;What would you like to focus on today?&quot;)

That experience should take less than 20 seconds, but over months it becomes the emotional anchor of the product.

Think of it as MITRA's equivalent of the Apple startup chime—not a sound, but a daily habit that users come to expect and appreciate.





The Final Page of Volume IV

I would end with this statement:

Good design is not when users admire the interface.

Good design is when they forget the interface exists because it quietly helps them do meaningful work.

MITRA succeeds when technology disappears and care remains.

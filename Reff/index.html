Why Mitra is Different

Traditional AI Chatbots

Entirely reactive. Waits for the warden to type structured questions. Offers generic internet answers.

The Ten Core Modules

Mitra is designed with a specialized framework divided into 10 structured, interconnected service modules.

01

Daily Conversations

A completely non-judgmental space for wardens to explore conflicts, daily events, or queries. Avoids cold instruction, uses open exploration.

Module 1 • Conversation
02

Hostel Management Assistant

Practical, real-time guidance on routines, discipline, nutrition, health, parental communication, and local facility cleanliness.

Module 2 • Operations
03

SEL Coaching

Aligned with CASEL competencies (Self-Awareness, Relationship Skills). Recommends journaling, breathing exercises, and active listening.

Module 3 • Emotional
04

Student Wellbeing Support

Deals proactively with homesickness, absenteeism, peer aggression, bullying, and hygiene without defaulting to standard punitive action.

Module 4 • Child Care
05

Critical Incident Guidance

Step-by-step guidance for extreme scenarios (missing child, fire, snake bites, abuse disclosure) detailing exactly who to contact.

Module 5 • Critical SOPs
06

Knowledge Companion

Instant, citation-backed access to Government circulars, child protection guidelines, MSMS training handbooks, and nutrition files.

Module 6 • Knowledge
07

Daily Digital Register

No complex structures. Wardens simply chat about what happened ("2 kids have fever..."), and Mitra structures the record automatically.

Module 7 • Registry Logs
08

Reflection Journal

Prompts weekly deep thoughts: "Who made you smile today?", "Which child needs more attention tomorrow?" promoting mindful practice.

Module 8 • Self-Mindfulness
09

Habit Builder

Keeps track of vital habits (hydration, sleeping on time, observing positive progress in one student) with weekly micro-challenges.

Module 9 • Habit Loops
10

Self-Care Companion

Ensures wardens check on their own mental status. Mitigates burnout and tracks local isolation-induced anxiety with grounding routines.

Module 10 • Wellbeing

Database Schema Model (PostgreSQL Tables)

Table: users & wardens

CREATE TABLE wardens (
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY REFERENCES auth.users(id),
  full_name TEXT NOT NULL,
  hostel_name TEXT NOT NULL,
  district TEXT NOT NULL,
  student_count INTEGER NOT NULL,
  gender_served TEXT CHECK (gender_served IN ('girls', 'boys', 'co-ed')),
  experience_level INTEGER,
  language_preference TEXT DEFAULT 'marathi'
);

Table: daily_logs & registers

CREATE TABLE daily_logs (
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
  warden_id UUID REFERENCES wardens(id),
  raw_input TEXT NOT NULL,
  synthesized_records JSONB, -- stores structured health, attendance, activity logs
  mood_index INTEGER CHECK (mood_index BETWEEN 1 AND 5),
  created_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT timezone('utc', now())
);

Crucial Pre-Development PM Questions

As Technical Architects, we must answer these critical ambiguities before releasing Phase 1:

1. Authentication Flow: How will wardens log in via WhatsApp/Telegram without complex user/pass credentials? We recommend linking phone numbers with simple OTP.
2. Offline Support Strategy: Ashram Schools are located in deep rural Maharashtra with spotty network. If there is no network, how do we buffer logs? We recommend offline SQLite sync cache on the MVP mobile container.
3. Safety & Escalation Triggering: If a warden reports an active emergency (like a Snake Bite), do we trigger direct automated SMS to District Coordinators? Yes, critical workflows need strict fail-safes.

Technical Architecture Recommendations

1. Hybrid Code-Mixed NLP Tokenizer

Instead of translating Marathi completely to English before reasoning, we suggest keeping fine-tuned custom embeddings on code-mixed words (e.g. "Hostel मध्ये", "mula fighting") to maintain context and emotional safety.

2. Low-Latency Voice Transcription Pipeline

Since wardens prefer voice messaging over typing, compress voice messages into highly packed WAV files to minimize cellular data consumption prior to uploading to Whisper API.
